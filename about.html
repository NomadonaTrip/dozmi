Who We Are

A legacy of faith & revival

At The Revival Center, we believe that revival is not just an event — it's a lifestyle. Our mission is to raise a generation that carries the fire of God and impacts families, communities, and nations for His glory.

We are Deliverers of Zion Ministries International (DOZMI) — a bible-believing community committed to the supremacy of God's word, the saving grace of Jesus Christ and the transformative power of the Holy Spirit.
