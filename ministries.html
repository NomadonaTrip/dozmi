Our Ministries & Outreach

Where you can serve & grow

At Deliverers of Zion Ministries International, we believe in equipping believers, serving communities, and fulfilling the Great Commission. Our ministries are designed to build faith, transform lives, and impact nations with the love and power of Christ.

For Our Children

Kids Church — raising godly champions

Proverbs 22:6

"Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it."

Children are not just the future of the church — they are an active part of God's kingdom today. At DOZMI, we believe in shaping young hearts with biblical truths, strong moral values, and a deep love for God.
